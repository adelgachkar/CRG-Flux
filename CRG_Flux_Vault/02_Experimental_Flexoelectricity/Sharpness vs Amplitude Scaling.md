# Sharpness vs Amplitude Scaling Dynamics

## 1. Non-Linear Curvature Transition
In linear flexoelectric models, polarization scales strictly linearly with macroscopic bending amplitude $A$. However, when the curvature wavelength $\lambda$ drops below the screening radius, a singular regime shift occurs:

$$\kappa_{\text{local}} \approx \frac{A}{\lambda^2} \gg \kappa_{\text{linear}}$$

| Parameter | Linear Regime ($\lambda \gg a_0$) | Singular Regime ($\lambda \to a_0$) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Polarization $\mathbf{P}$** | $\propto A / \lambda$ | $\propto A / \lambda^2$ |
| **Strain Gradient** | Smooth, continuous | Localized $\delta$-like spike |
| **Electronic Transport** | Ballistic conduction | Tunnelling / Field Emission |

## 2. Transition Point
The critical boundary $\lambda_c$ marks the onset of structural dislocation nucleation, where elastic deformation energy is directly converted into electrostatic potential energy.
